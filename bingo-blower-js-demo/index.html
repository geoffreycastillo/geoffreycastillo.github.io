Blower options

The values shown on load are the default values.

Width and height of the blower, which is a square, in pixels
Thickness of the walls of the blower, in pixels
Radius of the balls, in pixels
Denser balls are heavier and need a stronger windForce
How much the balls slow down when they slide against each other or the walls
Air resistance: how much the balls slow down as they move
How much force it takes to move a ball at rest
Bounciness of the balls
How strongly the air blows from the bottom of the blower
Colour of the target, the cross that appears in the middle when a ball is drawn
Length of each bar of the target cross
Thickness of each bar of the target cross
Colour of the circle around the ball drawn
Thickness of the circle around the ball drawn
How long the balls keep moving after the target appears, before the ball closest to it is drawn
How long the blower takes to go from blurry to sharp (0 for no blur)
frostedEdges
Blurs the edges of the blower and washes them out to white, so that the balls there cannot be counted, even from a screenshot
How much the frosted edges are blurred. Only used when frostedEdges is checked
How far the frosted edges reach into the blower, in pixels. Only used when frostedEdges is checked
How much the frosted edges are washed out to white, from 0 to 1. Only used when frostedEdges is checked

Minimal example

HTML:

<canvas id="world"></canvas>
<button type="button" id="draw">Draw a ball</button>
The ball drawn is: <span id="result"></span>

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/matter-js@0.20.0/build/matter.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/geoffreycastillo/bingo-blower-js@v1.3.0/bingo-blower.min.js"></script>

JS:

const blower = new BingoBlower();
blower.addBalls([18, 6, 3]);
document.getElementById('draw').addEventListener('click', async () => {
    const draw = await blower.drawBall();
    document.getElementById('result').textContent = draw.label;
});
